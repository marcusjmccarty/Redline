      A few bearings

      Add a city
      Type a name and press Enter. Use ↑ ↓ to choose a search result.
      Arrange the trip
      Click a route city to zoom to that stop. Drag its name to reorder, or focus it and use Alt + arrow keys. The chevron collapses the list.
      Trace
      Press Space to start or resume, and hold it for 2× speed. Click the trace button to pause. A full trace finishes at the world overview. Zoom during travel to follow the line at your chosen level.
      One city at a time
      Use the arrows beside Trace or your keyboard’s arrow keys. The camera gently follows the route and settles with both cities in view. Press again during travel to arrive instantly in the same view. The trip loops both ways through the overview: Back from the overview goes to the last city. Return, Home, or the globe glides back to the whole-world overview.
      Just the map
      Press H to hide the controls. H, Escape, or a double-click on the map brings them back.
      Keep your trips
      S saves your trip. T opens Trips: name this route, open any saved trip from its little map, or use ⋯ to rename, export, or delete one. Trips, photos, and postcard notes stay in this browser, with no account needed. Use Back up all regularly and Import to restore or move devices. Clearing website data or using private browsing can erase local saves. Backups contain your photos and notes; keep them private.
      Photos
      P opens the album. Turn its pages with the arrows, ← →, the page corners, or a swipe; the tabs on its edge jump to a city, and the pin finds that city on the map. All photos shows every photo at once, where Select moves or deletes several. Add photos, or drag them anywhere onto the page: photos with location data find their city, and you can assign the rest one by one or all at once. Escape closes the viewer or the album. Location metadata is read to suggest a city and removed from the stored photo. City locations: GeoNames.
      The viewer
      Use ← → or swipe to browse. Change the city under a photo to move it, or use the bin to delete it.
      Postcards
      Choose Postcard, press F, or click the photo to turn it over. Write on the back; it saves automatically. Print or Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on Mac) prints both sides. Share sends an image of both sides through your device’s share menu, or downloads it when file sharing is unavailable.
      The map
      Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Drag to pan when zoomed in. Country, city, and regional detail appears as you zoom.
