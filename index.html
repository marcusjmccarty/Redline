    A few bearings

    Add a city
    Type a name and press Enter. Use ↑ ↓ to choose a search result.
    Arrange the trip
    Click a route city to zoom to that stop. Drag its name to reorder, or focus it and use Alt + arrow keys. The chevron collapses the list.
    Trace
    Press Space or Enter to start or resume. Hold either for 2× speed. Click the trace button to pause. A full trace finishes at the world overview. Zoom during travel to follow the line at your chosen level.
    One city at a time
    Use the arrows beside Trace or your keyboard’s arrow keys. The camera gently follows the route and settles with both cities in view. Press again during travel to arrive instantly in the same view. The trip loops both ways through the overview: Back from the overview goes to the last city. Home or the globe returns to the overview.
    Just the map
    Press H to hide the controls. H, Escape, or a double-click on the map brings them back.
    Keep your trips
    S saves your trip. T opens or closes Trips. Trips, photos, and postcard notes stay in this browser, with no account needed. Use Back up all regularly and Import to restore or move devices. Clearing website data or using private browsing can erase local saves. Backups contain your photos and notes; keep them private.
    Photos
    P opens or closes Photos. Select photos and assign a city together, then save. Click a photo to expand it. Location metadata is read to suggest a city and removed from the stored photo.
    Postcards
    Click a photo or the postcard paper to flip it. Write on the back; it saves automatically. Print or Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on Mac) prints both sides. Share sends an image of both sides through your device’s share menu, or downloads it when file sharing is unavailable.
    The map
    Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Drag to pan when zoomed in. Country, city, and regional detail appears as you zoom.
