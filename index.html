        A few bearings

        Plan a trip
        Type a destination and press Enter. Use ↑ ↓ to choose a search result. In the logbook, drag a city’s name to reorder it (on a touch screen, drag its number), or focus it and use Alt + arrow keys. Use × beside a stop to remove it. Undo or Ctrl + Z (⌘ + Z on Mac) restores route edits, including a cleared route; Ctrl / ⌘ + Shift + Z redoes them.
        Trace route
        Press Return or Space to start or resume the trace, and hold either for 2.5× speed. The Trace route button pauses. A full trip finishes at the world overview. Zoom before or during travel to follow the line at your chosen level.
        One city at a time
        Click a city to look at it within the whole trip. The arrows beside Trace route, or your arrow keys, then travel from that city to the next or previous one; press again during travel to arrive at once. Backspace (Delete on a Mac), Home, or the globe glides back to the overview.
        Just the map
        Press H to hide the controls. H, Escape, or a double-click on the map brings them back.
        The logbook
        S saves the trip. L opens a lined logbook on the left: name the trip, drag stops to reorder them, or remove them. Switch trip at the top opens your other trips and their rename, export, and delete controls. Backed-up trips lists trips exported or imported since backup tracking began. Trips, photos, and postcards stay in this browser with no account; use Back up all regularly and Import backup to restore or move devices. Clearing website data or private browsing can erase them, and backups contain your photos and notes, so keep them private. Text you type into the destination box (two or more letters) is sent to Open-Meteo to find places, even when the place is also bundled.
        The album
        A opens the album to your last page. Press F to fill the window or return to the regular view; there, the window’s top edge brings up the toolbar and its right edge the city tabs. Click outside or press Escape to close the book. Turn pages with the arrows, ← →, the page corners, a swipe, or the slider; Home and End close it on either cover. The contents page and the tabs on its edge jump to a city, and the pin beside a city’s name finds it on the map. All photos shows every photo at once; press Select, then click photos (Shift-click selects a run) to move or delete them. Add photos, or drag them anywhere onto the page; photos with location data find their city. City locations: GeoNames.
        Photos and postcards
        Click a print to open it; browse its city with ← →, a swipe, or the arrows at the photo’s edges. Use Caption on the photo’s front to write the caption shown in the album. Captions save separately from postcard messages and are included in backups. Use the bin to delete a photo; All photos → Select also lets you move photos. Choose Postcard, press F, or click the photo to turn it over and write on the back; it saves automatically, and Save commits your changes immediately. Click outside or press Escape to close. Print or Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on Mac) prints both sides, and Share sends an image of both sides.
        The map
        Scroll, pinch, or use + / − to zoom. Double-click to zoom in, Shift + double-click to zoom out. Drag to pan when zoomed in; a flick coasts to a stop. Country, city, and regional detail appears as you zoom.
