      A few bearings

      Chart a course
      Type a port of call and press Enter. Use ↑ ↓ to choose a search result. Drag a port’s name to reorder it, or focus it and use Alt + arrow keys. The chevron folds the list away.
      Set sail
      Press Return or Space to start or resume the trace, and hold either for 2× speed. The Set sail button pauses. A full voyage finishes at the world overview. Zoom during travel to follow the line at your chosen level.
      One port at a time
      Click a port to look at it within the whole voyage. The arrows beside Set sail, or your arrow keys, then sail on from that port to the next or previous one; press again during travel to arrive at once. Backspace (Delete on a Mac), Home, or the globe glides back to the overview.
      Just the map
      Press H to hide the controls. H, Escape, or a double-click on the map brings them back.
      The logbook
      S seals (saves) the voyage. T opens the logbook inside the planner: name this voyage, open another from its little chart, or use ⋯ to rename, export, or delete one. Voyages, photos, and postcards stay in this browser with no account; use Back up all regularly and Import backup to restore or move devices. Clearing website data or private browsing can erase them, and backups contain your photos and notes, so keep them private.
      The album
      P opens the voyage’s album, from its front cover to its back cover. Turn pages with the arrows, ← →, the page corners, a swipe, or the slider; Home and End close it on either cover. The contents page and the tabs on its edge jump to a port, and the pin beside a port’s name finds it on the map. All photos shows every photo at once; press Select, then click photos (Shift-click selects a run) to move or delete them. Add photos, or drag them anywhere onto the page; photos with location data find their port. City locations: GeoNames.
      Photos and postcards
      Click a print to open it; browse its port with ← →, a swipe, or the arrows at the photo’s edges. Change the city under a photo to move it, or use the bin to delete it. Choose Postcard, press F, or click the photo to turn it over and write on the back; it saves automatically. Print or Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on Mac) prints both sides, and Share sends an image of both sides.
      The map
      Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Drag to pan when zoomed in. Country, city, and regional detail appears as you zoom.
